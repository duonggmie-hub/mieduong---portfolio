01 / Introduction

A little about me.

Hi, I’m Mie — a Toronto-based Product & Graphic Designer and an Advertising & Graphic Design graduate from Humber Polytechnic.

I've always enjoyed making things, from journaling and crafting to nail artistry. Working with my hands shaped how I think about colour, composition, and the little details that make an experience feel considered.

My background in marketing and visual design led me toward digital products. Today, I'm exploring how clear interfaces, thoughtful interactions, and strong visual systems can make everyday experiences better.
